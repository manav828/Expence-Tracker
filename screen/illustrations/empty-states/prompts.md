# ExpenCircle empty-state illustration prompts

The built-in image generator used the shared prompt followed by one subject prompt per file. Transparency was enabled for all four images.

## Shared prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single standalone compact spot illustration for the empty state of ExpenCircle, a modern finance tracker. Art direction: premium editorial 3D papercraft, dense simple silhouettes, restrained soft bevels, matte satin enamel and thick sculpted paper, subtle contact occlusion, slight three-quarter angle, sculptural and calm. Readable at 112px wide. ExpenCircle palette: dominant Imperial Pine Green #006948 and #004D34, a little #00855D, mint #85F8C4 and #E8F5E9, off-white #F5FFF7; use Electric Indigo #4B41E1 only as a very small deliberate accent, with Ocean Teal #008096 optional as a dark tonal accent. Square composition, one cohesive object grouping centered with generous even transparent padding. True transparent alpha outside the illustration, no solid background, no backdrop shapes, floor, cast shadow outside object, border, UI layout, hands, section headings, words, numbers, or watermark. Avoid coin piles, glitter, confetti, sparkles, glassmorphism, gradients across the canvas, and busy decoration.
```

## First transaction

```text
Subject: one softly curled pale paper receipt with a neat cut edge in front of a pine-green rounded transaction slip, with a small mint circular plus medallion tucked at the lower edge. The receipt has two thick abstract line marks and a clear negative-space shape, no typography. The plus is the only character. The concept is adding a first everyday transaction; the receipt should be the primary, clearest silhouette.
```

## Empty Photo Vault

Subject: a stack of exactly two compact instant-print photo cards, the foremost displaying simplified pine-green hills and a mint sun with a pale paper frame. A small clock medallion overlaps a lower corner to suggest time-limited storage. A slight angle separates the two cards. No camera, lock, labels, numerals or additional props. The photo stack is the clear primary silhouette.
